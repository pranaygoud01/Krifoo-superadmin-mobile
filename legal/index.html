1. Scope and Applications

This Privacy Policy sets forth the principles under which Krifoo ("we", "us", or "our") governs the collection, recording, storage, and processing of personal data. This document applies to all users interacting with our mobile applications and related web services:

  • Krifoo Mobile: Consumer client application for iOS (Bundle ID: com.krifoo.mobile) and Android (Package Name: com.krifoo.mobile). Used by customers to view restaurant menus, execute table reservations, place takeaway and delivery orders, and monitor order progression.
  • Krifoo Admin: Merchant client application for iOS (Bundle ID: com.krifoo.admin) and Android (Package Name: com.krifoo.admin). Used by licensed restaurant operators and kitchen personnel to accept orders, manage catalog availability, organize seating, and execute point-of-sale receipt printing.
  • Web Applications & APIs: Hosted at https://krifoo.co.uk.
Notice on Data Monetization

Krifoo does not sell, lease, commercialize, or rent consumer personal data or device geolocation coordinates to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or marketing intermediaries.

2. Categories of Data Collected

We process personal data provided directly by users and technical telemetry generated through system interaction:

2.1 Customer Profile & Transaction Data (Krifoo Mobile)

  • Identification Records: Full legal name, verified mobile contact number, email address, and optional avatar photograph.
  • Fulfillment Addresses: Delivery locations, street designations, postal codes, and specific courier access instructions.
  • Transaction Histories: Item line-orders, dining reservation schedules, special dietary annotations, subtotal and tax amounts, and timestamps.
  • Payment Information: Encrypted payment tokens. Payment card numbers, expiration dates, and CVVs are handled exclusively by Level 1 PCI-DSS certified payment processors. Krifoo does not retain unencrypted payment credentials.
  • Communications & Feedback: Support inquiries, ratings, reviews, and damaged item reports submitted through support channels.

2.2 Commercial Partner Data (Krifoo Admin)

  • Commercial Entity Records: Registered restaurant business name, operating premises address, manager contact details, and corporate identifiers.
  • Catalog & Operational Content: Menu specifications, pricing matrices, dish availability statuses, table capacities, and kitchen schedules.
  • Hardware Identifiers: Bluetooth hardware addresses, local network IP addresses, and printer specifications (e.g., Epson TM-m30III, ESC/POS hardware) utilized for thermal ticket printing.

2.3 Telemetry & Technical Metrics

  • Device Diagnostics: Hardware manufacturer, device model, operating system build, network operator, and network IP addresses.
  • Performance Data: Application crash stack traces, API response latencies, and system event logs.

4. Processing Purposes

Personal data is processed under contractual necessity, statutory obligation, and legitimate commercial interests:

  1. Order Fulfillment: Relaying order details to merchant kitchens, facilitating delivery routing, and confirming table reservations.
  2. Account Verification: Authenticating user sessions via One-Time Passcodes (OTP) and defending against automated credential abuse.
  3. Operational Communications: Issuing transactional receipts, order progression updates, and resolving customer service requests.
  4. Merchant Operations: Powering kitchen dispatch screens, automated thermal ticket printing, and sales accounting.
  5. Legal & Statutory Compliance: Adhering to applicable taxation, accounting standards, and food product traceability laws.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

Data is transferred only to authorized processors bound by data protection obligations:

  • Partner Restaurants: Merchant kitchens receive only the item specifications, recipient first name, phone number, and physical delivery address necessary for fulfillment.
  • Geographic Information Services: Google Maps Platform API is utilized to translate coordinates and calculate transit times under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Push Notification Gateways: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Expo Push Service are utilized to deliver real-time operational notifications.
  • Payment Gateways: Certified PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processors receive tokenized payment authorizations.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosures made strictly when required by enforceable judicial orders or statutory mandates.

6. Data Retention Schedules

  • Active Profile Data: Maintained for the duration of active account registration.
  • Fiscal & Transaction Records: Preserved for up to six (6) years following transaction execution in compliance with statutory taxation and accounting laws.
  • Transit Telemetry: GPS coordinates are processed transiently in memory and discarded upon order completion.
  • Account Deletion Requests: Personal profile identifiers are permanently erased from production databases within thirty (30) days of verified request receipt.

7. Account & Data Erasure (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) Compliant)

Users hold the right to obtain the permanent erasure of their account records and personal data:

Erasure Procedures
  1. Direct In-App Erasure: Navigate to Profile → Personal Info → Delete Account within Krifoo Mobile.
  2. Web Submission: Utilize the Account Deletion Tab within this portal.
  3. Written Request: Issue an email to admin@krifoo.co.uk with the subject header "Account Deletion Request".

8. Security Safeguards

We implement administrative, logical, and physical security measures. Network communications utilize TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 encryption. Production databases are encrypted at rest with AES-256. Database access is strictly confined to authorized systems engineers under least-privilege principles.

9. Protection of Minors

Our services are not directed to individuals under thirteen (13) years of age (or under sixteen (16) within the UK and European Economic Area). We do not knowingly solicit or maintain personal data from minors. In the event an unauthorized minor account is identified, it will be deleted immediately upon notification.

10. Statutory Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Subject to jurisdiction, data subjects may exercise statutory rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability without discriminatory treatment. Requests may be directed to our compliance desk at admin@krifoo.co.uk.

11. Amendments

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect procedural, regulatory, or technical changes. Material modifications will be accompanied by in-app notices or direct email communication prior to the effective date.

6. Cancellations, Refunds & Claims

6.1 Immediate Cancellation Window

6.2 Defective or Missing Items

Notice of missing or physically damaged goods must be logged via Order History within two (2) hours of receipt, accompanied by photographic evidence. Confirmed claims will be resolved via item replacement, merchant credit, or monetary reimbursement within 3 to 7 banking days.

Account Deletion & Data Erasure Procedure

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play Data Safety policies, users may request the permanent erasure of their account credentials and associated personal data.

Notice on Irreversibility

Executing an account erasure request is permanent. Saved delivery addresses, account preferences, order histories, table reservation logs, and reward balances cannot be restored once purged.

Method A: Direct In-App Erasure (Instant)

Follow these steps on a registered mobile device:

  1. Open the Krifoo Mobile Client.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab on the primary navigation bar.
  3. Select Personal Info.
  4. Scroll to the base of the interface and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the prompt. Current authentication sessions will be terminated and database records queued for deletion.

Method B: Web Erasure Request Form

For users who no longer have the client installed:

Request Received. A confirmation request has been prepared for dispatch to our compliance desk. Account deletion will be completed within 30 days of ownership verification.

Method C: Email Written Notice

Send an email from your registered account to admin@krifoo.co.uk with the subject header "Account Deletion Request".

Data Retention Schedule

  • Permanently Erased: Account authentication profiles, saved addresses, notification device tokens, customer avatar images.
  • Anonymized: Aggregated sales transaction statistics with all personal identifiers removed.
  • Statutorily Retained: Accounting records and tax invoices retained for up to six (6) years under legal fiscal requirements.
