## Full release notes

### Dine-in and tables
- **Live Floor.** A full-screen view of every table on every floor. You can see who is seated, what they ordered and how long they have been there. Seat a walk-in or a reservation, add orders, mark them served, move or join tables, ask for the bill and close it with one or more payments (cash, card, other). Updates live. To stop accidental exits mid-service, leaving needs a press and hold.
- **Floor setup.** Create floors and tables (name, seats, shape: square, round, long or booth). Drag tables onto the floor plan.
- **Table QR codes.** Each table has its own QR code. Guests scan it in the Krifoo app to order to that table. Codes print as an A4 sheet.
- **POS table picker.** Dine-in orders from the POS pick a real table from the live floor. A free table is seated automatically when the order is placed. You can still type a table name for tables that are not on the plan.
- **Tables & floor report** in Analytics: visits, guests, average time at the table, order-to-served time, spend per guest and busiest hours.

### Table bookings
- **Booking hours & rules.** Set weekly booking hours once (up to 4 time ranges a day), plus the longest booking, how far ahead guests can book, the notice needed and the pricing (free, deposit or per hour). Add date exceptions for closures or different hours.
- **Tables set up once.** Tables no longer have to be posted day by day. Restaurants using the old daily posts can import them in one tap.
- **Reservations day view.** A timeline with tables down the side and hours across. Tap a free slot to add a phone or walk-in booking. Booking actions are: call, seat now, move, no-show, completed and cancel. Cancelling refunds any fee automatically.

### Orders and kitchen
- **Kitchen Mode.** A full-screen New / Cooking / Ready board for kitchen staff. "Start cooking" accepts the order and prints the kitchen ticket if that is turned on. Items can be ticked off one by one, and there is an undo window.
- **Live order countdown.** Accepted ASAP orders count down to their expected time.
- **Action needed.** The home screen lists orders that are past their expected or scheduled time.
- **Edit expected time** from the Orders board or the order details screen.
- **Redesigned Orders and Menu tabs** with a shared filter and sort sheet that shows how many results match.
- **Veg / non-veg / egg symbols** on dishes in the menu editor and POS.

### Home and marketing
- **New home dashboard.** Quick actions, live order counts per channel (dine-in, takeaway, delivery) and recent orders.
- **Marketing posts.** Create announcements, or offers with a promo code (percentage or fixed amount, applied to all dishes, chosen categories or chosen dishes, with a valid-from and valid-to date). A live preview shows what customers will see.
- **Krifoo updates.** A new inbox for service messages from Krifoo, such as maintenance notices and new features. It opens from the notification feed or from a push notification.

### Printing and receipts
- **Redesigned printer settings** on one screen: printer status and test print, automatic printing, paper and copies, cash drawer and receipt design.
- **Receipt designer with exact preview.** The preview shows the receipt exactly as it will print.
- **More reliable printing** over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi/LAN, Epson and Sunmi printers. Staff are alerted when an automatic print fails, and orders are not printed twice after the app restarts.
- **Bluetooth permissions** are handled correctly on Android 12 and later.

### Store settings
- **Address finder.** Search for the store's real address to set an exact map location. Delivery distances and fees are worked out from it.
- **Logo picker.** Upload, change or remove the restaurant logo. The logo is also used on the ordering website.
- Redesigned settings, store profile, registration, opening hours, delivery settings and new-order sound settings.

### Super Admin
- **New overview dashboard.** Sales, earnings and growth compared with the previous period, plus what needs attention (late orders, approvals, payout setup, quiet stores).
- **Store sales.** Every store's sales in one list, with search, filters (selling, no sales, needs help) and sorting.
- **Payouts.** See what each restaurant is owed for card orders and record payouts, with an optional bank reference.
- **Restaurant updates.** Send push notifications to restaurant devices (info, important or maintenance), with a test send and the expected reach.
- **Email campaigns.** Build a campaign in three steps (audience, content, review and send) with a test email, and manage the contact list.

### Fixes
- General bug fixes and stability improvements.
- Removed unused images to make the app smaller.
